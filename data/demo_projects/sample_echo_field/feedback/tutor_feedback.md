# Echo Field — Tutor Feedback

## Context

This feedback was received after a rehearsal of the responsive visual installation.

## Feedback point 1 — Connect colour to movement

The visual response should not rely only on changes in speed. Colour should also change with movement intensity so that users receive more than one form of visual feedback.

A possible direction was to use blue for lower movement and shift towards green or yellow-green as movement increases.

## Feedback point 2 — Explain the change in interaction method

The project presentation should explain why the interaction changed from hand gestures to whole-body movement.

The final research framing should show that this decision emerged from practical experimentation with unstable gesture detection rather than presenting whole-body movement as an unexplained starting point.

## Feedback point 3 — Consider multiple participants

The difference between one-person and multi-person interaction could be explored.

Multiple bodies may produce a stronger movement signal, but this does not necessarily mean that each participant would feel an equal sense of control.

## Response to feedback

The colour system was connected to movement intensity.

The project documentation was revised to explain the transition from hand gestures to whole-body movement.

Multi-person interaction was retained as a possible future investigation rather than being added to the final prototype scope.
