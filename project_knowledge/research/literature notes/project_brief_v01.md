# ProjectOS — Project Brief v0.1

Date: 14 September 2026
Status: Working draft for research and supervisor discussion

## 1. Project Overview

ProjectOS is a proposed RAG-based creativity support tool for creative students or practitioners working on long-term creative projects.

It will use project documents and development records to help users review previous work, understand design decisions, reflect on progress and consider their next steps.

## 2. Initial Target Users and Context

The initial focus is creative students working on individual projects over several weeks or months.

These projects may involve briefs, research notes, tutor feedback, testing records and development logs. The shared activity is developing and revising a creative project over time. The initial study will focus on a manageable group within this wider audience.

## 3. Motivation and Problem to Investigate

The idea is informed by my experience of developing Echo Field. During that project, I needed to connect user feedback, tutor advice and technical experiments to decide what to change and explain my decisions.

This experience raises a question about how project records can support ongoing creative work. As documents and decisions accumulate, students may find it difficult to recover the context of earlier work and identify what still needs attention.

This is an initial assumption that I will investigate through relevant literature and user research.

## 4. Working Research Question

How can a RAG-based tool support project continuity and reflection for creative students working on long-term creative projects?

In this project, continuity means being able to recover previous project context and use it when continuing work. Reflection means reviewing decisions, feedback and experiments to understand what has been learned and what to explore next.

## 5. Proposed Core Interaction

A user uploads a project brief, feedback and development notes. They ask a question about their project, inspect the sources behind the answer and consider possible next steps.

The user can accept, edit or reject suggestions, record their progress and return later to review what has changed.

The initial prototype will include:

- Project document upload.
- Questions and answers with source references.
- Support for identifying and recording next steps.
- Progress logging.
- Project review and reflection.

## 6. Research and Evaluation Approach

I plan to use a practice-based approach, combining prototype development, two rounds of testing and revision, and written reflection.

Technical evaluation will examine whether the system retrieves relevant information and produces answers supported by its sources.

User evaluation will explore how people use the tool, whether it helps them review their projects and what difficulties they encounter. The study design and recruitment will follow the approved ethics arrangements.

Short-term evaluation will provide initial evidence of support and usability. It will not establish improvements in long-term creative outcomes.

## 7. Intended Contribution

The project aims to produce a working prototype and practical findings about designing document-based AI support for creative projects.

The research will examine where this support is useful, where it fails and how users retain control over their own creative decisions.
