# Day 1 Research Notes

Date: 14 September 2026
Project: ProjectOS
Focus: Reflecting on Echo Field to identify a possible user need

## 1. What project was I working on?

I was developing Echo Field, an interactive visual project using TouchDesigner and a webcam. The system detected body movement through changes in the camera image and used this information to change the visuals. I wanted users to notice a clear difference between different levels of movement.

## 2. What specific difficulty did I encounter?

During testing, users experienced different problems. Some felt that slow movements did not produce a clear response. Others noticed sudden changes in the visuals or thought that the colours and interaction needed improvement. I needed to consider this feedback alongside the technical limitations of the system and decide which problems to address first.

## 3. What did I do to address it?

I recorded feedback from five testers. During development, I adjusted how the movement values were processed and smoothed. Later, following my tutor's advice, I added clearer colour changes to help distinguish different movement levels. I also documented the development process and the remaining problems in my weblog.

## 4. What could be improved about this process?

These changes involved user feedback, tutor advice, technical experiments and visual results. To reflect on the project, I needed to connect these records to explain why I made particular decisions and which problems remained unresolved. This made me wonder whether project records could do more than document the process: could they also help me think about and plan the next stage?

## 5. How would I want ProjectOS to help?

I would like to upload my project brief, testing feedback and development logs to ProjectOS. I could then ask questions such as: “Which problems were mentioned repeatedly?”, “What changes have I already tried?” and “Which problems are still unresolved?”

The system could organise the relevant information, show its sources and suggest possible next steps. I would check the information, revise the suggestions where needed and make the final decisions myself.
