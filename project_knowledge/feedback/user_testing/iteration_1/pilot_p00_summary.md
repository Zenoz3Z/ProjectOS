# P00 pilot summary

**Date:** 5 October 2026\
**Prototype:** Baseline v0.1, commit `781f94d`\
**Session:** P00 pilot; excluded from the formal participant sample.

## Reported outcome

The participant ultimately completed all pilot tasks. No technical errors occurred.

The participant found the interface visually underdeveloped. The interaction instructions were not sufficiently explicit, and the amount and organisation of information made the interface feel complex. Task completion therefore does not mean that the experience was effortless.

## Interpretation and next iteration

The core workflow was usable in this pilot, but completing it involved substantial effort to understand the interface. The finding justifies a **Pre-test interaction refinement** using progressive disclosure and clearer guidance: a short introduction, one section at a time, optional evidence details, clearer review choices, explicit saving and navigation, and visible progress.

This refinement concerns presentation and interaction. The six-section research structure, retrieval and summarisation pipeline, review actions, JSON schema and participant data isolation are to remain unchanged.

## Limits

No research conclusions should be drawn from one pilot. These observations do not establish usability improvements, summary accuracy or benefits for project re-entry. The refined interaction still requires testing. P00 is excluded from the formal participant sample.
