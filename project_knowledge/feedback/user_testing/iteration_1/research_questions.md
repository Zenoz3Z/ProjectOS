# Round 1 research questions

## Core question

> How do source visibility and user correction help creative practitioners assess an AI-generated reconstruction of a long-term project during project re-entry?

The question allows negative and mixed answers. Visible sources or correction controls may help, add work, or fail to prevent a misleading interpretation; benefit is not assumed.

## Supporting questions

1. **Source visibility and evidence absence:** When do practitioners consult sources, what do they regard as adequate support, and how do they respond when evidence is missing, historical, resolved, conditional or wrongly attributed?
2. **Correction and control:** How and why do practitioners Confirm, Update or Reject a reconstruction, and can they express their own understanding without confusing it with original evidence?
3. **Re-entry and checking costs:** What clarity or practical help does the review provide, what verification effort does it require, and which interpretations might be misleading if accepted?

The [question and observation guide](post_test_questionnaire_and_interview.md) maps pre-test questions, tasks, seven Likert items, five interview questions and observation fields to these constructs.

## Proposed study

Three adults each bring one paused long-term creative project, represented by 4–8 self-selected, screened, non-sensitive files. The researcher imports the pack and prepares summaries after consent. Each 40–50 minute supervised local session starts with an independent written account, followed by an Echo Field tutorial, the six-section assigned-project review, ratings and a 10–15 minute interview.

Use descriptive statistics and thematic analysis; do not claim statistical significance, perfect accuracy, superiority to another tool or improved creativity/productivity. The participant's initial account and later corrections are perspectives to analyse, not unquestionable ground truth.

P00 is an excluded pilot; P96 is an excluded researcher self-test. Their records do not become part of the three-person formal sample, and P96 review counts are not formal study results. Formal P01 has not started.

## Scope and readiness

Automatic temporal reasoning, automatic contradiction detection, self-service upload, public deployment, user accounts and perfect reconstruction accuracy are non-goals. [TD-003](../../../decisions/technical_decisions/td_003_freeze_automatic_project_state_inference.md) freezes retrieval expansion; explicit temporal metadata remains a possible post-study interface direction or future work.

The [Round 1 protocol](study_protocol_round_1_draft.md) supersedes earlier demo-only and five-to-eight-person proposals for this round. Historical plans remain available as dated records and in Git history. All current study documents remain drafts until Anna confirms scope and the required ethics route is completed. Do not recruit or collect project files before that point.
