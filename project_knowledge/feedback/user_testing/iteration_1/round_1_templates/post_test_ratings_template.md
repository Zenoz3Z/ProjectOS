## Researcher interpretation

R2 records participant-assessed alignment, not objective accuracy or ground truth. R6 is a negative-direction item: higher agreement indicates more verification effort than expected. Do not combine R6 directly with positive items without explaining direction and method; this draft uses separate item-level descriptions and no composite score. Preserve missing/N/A responses, report denominators, and do not claim statistical significance.
