ProjectOS saved actions and correction text are the authoritative review records. This sheet is auxiliary **researcher observation**; it does not capture every click or provide a complete interaction log. Reconcile the action field with the latest saved formal review at the recorded endpoint, and retain revisions separately. Tutorial activity is excluded.

Record only observable behaviour and what the participant explicitly says. Do not infer mental states or treat a long pause as evidence of confusion. Where possible describe a pause's approximate duration/context without inventing a diagnostic threshold. Keep direct wording distinct from paraphrase.

Use Yes/No only when observed or verifiable; leave unobserved fields blank with a note. Evidence opening explicitly allows Uncertain. No means a determinate observation, not a missed event. An opening does not prove reading or understanding. For the matrix, count distinct sections with an observed opening only when all six observations are determinate; this is not a total click count.

Do not copy source text or correction content into this observation sheet. Use private saved review references if needed. Mark suspected misunderstandings as such unless the participant's statement establishes them.
