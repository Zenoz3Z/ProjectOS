- [ ] I consent to my personal data being processed for this research as described in the participant information and privacy notice.
- [ ] I agree to provide 4–8 self-selected, screened, non-sensitive records from one paused long-term creative project that I am authorised to share. I may withhold or redact records before providing them.

- [ ] I understand the session takes approximately 40–50 minutes on the researcher's computer; pack preparation occurs beforehand.
- [ ] I agree to the independent written project account, six-section review, short seven-item questionnaire and interview, while retaining my right to skip activities.

## Data storage and withdrawal

| Data stored | Where it is stored | How long it is kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participant-selected project files, extracted text and temporary working copies | A restricted UAL OneDrive folder and, only where necessary for preparation or the supervised session, the researcher's password-protected computer | Permanently deleted within 7 calendar days after the session |
| De-identified research records: AI-generated summaries, displayed source excerpts, review choices, corrections, ratings, interview notes and observation notes; also the written pre-test account, reasons and timings collected in the same session | Restricted UAL OneDrive | Retained until 30 November 2027 and then permanently deleted |
| Signed consent forms | Restricted UAL OneDrive, separately from research records | Retained until 30 November 2027 and then permanently deleted |
| Temporary identity-to-participant-code mapping | Restricted UAL OneDrive, separately from research records and signed consent forms | Deleted after the 7-day withdrawal period |
| Text sent to OpenAI, an external service | OpenAI's service; participant names and contact details will not deliberately be sent | Abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days, subject to the external-service limits explained in the information sheet |

Temporary copies used on the computer will be removed within the same 7-day period; the retained de-identified research records will be held in restricted UAL OneDrive. The temporary identity link is deleted after the 7-day withdrawal period. After deletion of that link and aggregation, withdrawal may no longer be possible. These statements remain subject to wording review at Meeting 3.
