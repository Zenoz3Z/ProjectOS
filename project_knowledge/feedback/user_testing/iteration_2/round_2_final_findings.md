# Round 2 final findings

**Final descriptive findings for the completed Round 2 study**

Study closure recorded on 24 October 2026. Round 2 consisted of P03 and P04, two participants. There is no P05 and no further participant recruitment is planned. Final describes the closed dataset, not statistical certainty or full prototype validation.

Both participants described their projects as complete or largely complete. The results are descriptive and exploratory. Detailed responses, individual rating profiles and project materials remain private.

## Main findings

1. **Non-applicability represented completed-project states.** The added response was used when a participant no longer recognised an active aim, issue or next step, as well as for absent information. These meanings are not interchangeable, and nine uses alone do not demonstrate interface improvement.
2. **No interface-operation problem was observed in the two sessions.** This supports a bounded usability observation for the tested contexts. It does not establish a causal effect of the refinement or usability for all users.
3. **Visible sources did not automatically produce frequent checking.** Only two of twelve sections had observed evidence opening. Perceived source usefulness must remain distinct from active verification.
4. **Historical records did not necessarily describe participant-recognised current state.** Completed projects created a temporal distinction between past aims or limitations and what participants considered relevant now. Non-applicability does not erase historical evidence.
5. **Generic insufficient messages reduced some perceived usefulness.** One session account associated the limited number of populated summaries with a less useful overall experience. An insufficient label alone does not distinguish genuinely absent material from evidence the system missed.
6. **Missing information needs a more actionable explanation.** One participant explicitly requested guidance about which document or information types were missing. This is a future-work implication, not a feature implemented in the frozen prototype or a request documented from both participants.
7. **Changes and reasons was useful or considered accurate in both sessions.** Both participants accepted Section 2 as reasonably aligned; one identified it as most helpful. This is participant-assessed alignment rather than independent factual verification.
8. **An output that looks appropriate can coexist with incomplete evidence selection.** An existing preparation diagnosis identified missed evidence, while later non-applicability choices could fit a completed-project perspective. That juxtaposition does not validate the retrieval pathway or prove why each response was chosen. Technical diagnosis is kept separate from participant findings.

## Limitations

- Only two participants and two different projects.
- Both projects were complete or largely complete; the round lacked a clearly paused but still active project that the participant intended to resume.
- Researcher-organised source packs and retrospective self-report.
- Limited, manually observed evidence expansion rather than a complete interaction log.
- No comparison condition or basis for causal claims about the interface.
- Ambiguous interpretation of P03 R6 and some non-applicability reasons.
- Existing reviews are not durably linked to exact generated-summary snapshots; current cache inspection cannot prove every past display.
- Findings cannot be generalised to all long-term creative projects.

## Conclusion and record status

These two sessions showed workable interface operation, frequent Not applicable responses and limited evidence checking. Content coverage and the interpretation of absence affected usefulness. The distinction between documented past state and participant-recognised current state remains central.

Round 2 is closed. Prototype feature development is frozen; remaining work is final analysis review, thesis integration, final validation and submission preparation. No further recruitment or missing-document-guidance implementation is planned.
