### 3. Drafted the evaluation plan

I created:

`project_knowledge/current_state/evaluation_plan_v0.md`

The provisional plan proposes a maximum of eight adult participants across two stages:

- three formative participants for Iteration 1;
- five new participants for Iteration 2.

The proposed primary material is a prepared common project archive.

This would allow all participants to encounter the same:

- current intention;
- project change;
- resolved feedback;
- unresolved blocker;
- superseded direction;
- and next action.

Participants’ own projects may be discussed as a secondary activity if the supervisor considers this useful and ethically appropriate.

### 4. Defined evaluation evidence

The provisional evaluation would collect:

- sections opened;
- evidence opened;
- Confirm, Correct and Reject actions;
- correction text;
- task completion time;
- project-state answers;
- short rating responses;
- and semi-structured interview data.

The evaluation is intended to examine:

- project-state clarity;
- evidence use;
- correction and agency;
- readiness to resume the project;
- and the effort required to review the system.

The current recommendation is a task-based qualitative study rather than an underpowered experimental comparison.

## Reflection

Today’s work shifted the focus from building more software to clarifying what the existing prototype demonstrates.

The most important argument remains that semantic relevance and project-state accuracy are not the same.

The prototype failure in the Unresolved Tensions section provides concrete evidence for this argument.

ProjectOS retrieved semantically relevant tutor feedback but could not reliably determine whether that feedback was unresolved, implemented or historical.

The correction workflow then demonstrated the proposed response to this limitation: the user can inspect, correct and preserve the reconstruction without removing the original evidence.

Preparing the meeting materials also exposed that the evaluation method is now more important than additional functionality.

The project already contains enough technical structure to begin formal iteration once the research and evaluation scope are confirmed.
