## Day 1 Research Framing and Literature Synthesis

Today I reviewed and synthesised literature across four areas: creativity support tools, reflective AI, retrieval-augmented generation, and support for revisiting creative work.

My initial project idea was a broad RAG-based assistant for managing long-term creative projects. The literature helped me recognise that project management alone would not provide a sufficiently specific creative practice. I therefore narrowed the project towards project re-entry: how creative students return to an ongoing project across working sessions and reconsider earlier design decisions.

Existing research already connects project histories, documentation, reflection, and creative exploration. Recent systems such as Reflexa and IdeaBlocks also show that AI-supported reflection and the reuse of creative intentions are not entirely new. This means that ProjectOS cannot claim novelty simply by combining RAG with reflection.

My current research opportunity is more specific. I want to investigate whether source-grounded project records can help users reconstruct and reassess earlier creative decisions without allowing the AI to replace the user’s interpretation. The system should separate original evidence, AI-generated interpretations, and user-confirmed decisions.

The provisional research question is:

How can a RAG-based tool support creative students in reconstructing and reassessing prior design decisions when they return to an ongoing project across working sessions?

My next step is to confirm this scope with my supervisor and begin designing the smallest complete interaction for the first prototype.

### Decisions made

- The project will focus on project re-entry rather than general project management.
- RAG will be treated as an implementation method rather than the research contribution.
- The first prototype will use text-based project records.
- Source evidence, AI interpretation, and user decisions will remain separate.
- The project will not attempt to prove long-term creativity improvement.
- The prototype will contain one complete workflow followed by two evidence-led upgrades.
